Good to know

  • Download the language packs for your two languages before you travel. The first download needs an internet connection. Once the packs are installed, Tripsay works in Airplane Mode.
  • Translations appear as text. Tripsay does not read them aloud.
  • Put the phone between the two of you, close enough to hear both. In noisy places, or with very short replies, it may miss or mishear words.
  • To show the latest translation over other apps, tap the floating window button while listening.
